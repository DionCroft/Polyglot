# Read along with a rolling transcript

Start a lecture as usual, then open the **Transcript** tab. Each completed passage
stays on screen with its timestamp, original speech and translation. The original
language is labelled **spoken** and the other language **translation**. Mandarin
questions and English answers share the same history.

## Read earlier text without stopping the lecture

The view starts in **Following live** mode. Scroll upwards to read earlier passages;
listening and translation continue. **Back to live · 3 new**, for example, means
three new passages have arrived while you were reading. Click **Back to live** to
return to the newest text. Selecting text or searching also stops automatic following.

**Pause** is different: it stops listening and hides the projector captions. Use it
for private conversations. Finished passages stay in the transcript, and a translation
of already recognised speech may finish while paused. Unfinished speech is excluded.

## Find and copy

- Enter an English or Chinese word in **Find English or Chinese text…**, then press
  **Enter** or **Next**. **Previous** searches backwards. Search wraps around at the end
  and is not case-sensitive. The current match is selected.
- Use **Ctrl+F** on Windows or **Command+F** on Mac while inside the transcript to focus search.
- Select any text with the mouse and choose **Copy selection**, or use **Ctrl+C** on
  Windows / **Command+C** on Mac.
- **Copy transcript** copies all completed passages currently held in this view,
  including timestamps, language labels and any explicit missing-speech notices.
  Unfinished speech at the bottom is not included.

Example: after explaining the critical path, scroll back or search **critical path**.
Let a student ask “请再解释一下关键路径。” in Mandarin. Their question and its English
translation are added while you read. Select **Back to live** before answering.

## Make the projector easy to read

Under **Overlay → Caption layout**, select **Rolling · readable projector** (the new default).
It shows recent passages with English and Simplified Chinese in aligned columns.
Text size stays fixed; older text moves out of the visible area instead of shrinking.
Long passages can extend above the visible area; open **Transcript** for their full text.
Use **Text size**, **Overlay width**, **Rolling overlay height** and **Line spacing** to
fit your room. Choose the caption display, preview it and check readability from the
back of the room. A wider or taller panel fits more text.

The rolling projector displays completed recognised phrases as they arrive; translation
follows in the same row. The unfinished phrase remains visible in the full controls and
Transcript tab. **Compact · latest caption** keeps the previous overlay behaviour,
including provisional text and automatic size fitting. English-only and Chinese-only
display options still work. Lock either layout before teaching so clicks reach your slides.

## Saving and privacy

**Save text transcripts and subtitles** still controls writing text to disk. The rolling
view works when saving is off, using temporary memory only. Copy anything you need before
starting another lecture or closing the app. Finishing a lecture leaves the history visible;
starting a new lecture clears it. Changing language or pausing does not clear finished passages.
Clipboard contents are managed by your operating system after you choose Copy.

To bound memory during very long sessions, the reader retains the most recent **10,000
passages** (about fourteen hours at one passage every five seconds). It tells you if older
passages leave the view. Search and copy cover the retained passages. When saving is on,
the separate transcript files and recovery journal retain the complete session; find them
under **Diagnostics → Open transcripts**. Audio is not recorded.

**Translating…**, **Translation unavailable** and **Speech not transcribed** identify pending
work or gaps. They are not guesses about what someone said. Recognition models, English
vocabulary/CO7000 hints, Auto language thresholds and acceleration settings are unchanged.
