# Mandarin to English translation model

LectureLive 0.6 uses **Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-zh-en**, developed by the University of
Helsinki Language Technology Research Group, in the ONNX conversion published by
Xenova. It is licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International**.

- Original model and authors: https://huggingface.co/Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-zh-en
- ONNX conversion: https://huggingface.co/Xenova/opus-mt-zh-en

The original model was converted to ONNX and quantised by the conversion publisher.
LectureLive uses the published quantised weights without further training or weight changes.

services for the World”, Proceedings of the 22nd Annual Conference of the European
Association for Machine Translation, 2020.
